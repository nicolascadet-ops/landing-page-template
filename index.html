      Questions, answered.

      Something else? Email hello@flowdesk.example and a human replies the same day.

      Do I need to change my support email address?

      No. Keep support@yourcompany.com. You forward it to Flowdesk (or connect Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 directly) and replies still go out from your own address.

      How does Flowdesk decide who gets an email?

      First your rules, in the order you set them. If no rule matches, Flowdesk suggests a tag from the email's content and assigns it to the teammate with the lightest queue in that team. You can always reassign, and it learns from that.

      Is our customers' data used to train AI models?

      No. Email content is processed only to route your own inbox and is never used to train shared models. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

      What happens when the trial ends?

      You choose a plan, or your account pauses. Nothing is deleted for 30 days, so you can come back without losing your rules or history.

      Can we import our history from another help desk?

      Yes. Import from a CSV export or connect your old inbox and Flowdesk pulls in the last 12 months of conversations.
